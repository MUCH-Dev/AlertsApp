What's new

Four updates to how you track, sort, and act on alerts.

This round of changes is aimed at two things: keeping the list current without babysitting it, and letting you act on an alert without leaving its detail card. Here's what's changing and why it should make the day-to-day a bit smoother.

Live now

The list keeps itself current

Alerts and the summary tiles at the top now refresh on their own every fifteen seconds, so a teammate's status change, snooze, or assignment shows up without you reloading the page. If you've got a card open or alerts checked off, it holds off until you're done — nothing gets refreshed out from under you mid-task.

Every column in Grid view now sorts

Client Code, Vendor, Status, Assigned, Last Bill, Days on Alert — click any header to sort by it, click again to reverse the order. Handy for lining up everyone's oldest bills, or grouping by who's carrying what.

Snooze and Bill Pulled, right from the card

Open an alert's detail card and you'll now find Snooze and Mark Bill Pulled sitting alongside Save — no more popping back out to the list to do it. Marked Bill Pulled by mistake? It's a real toggle now, so you can switch it back without touching the checkbox that used to hide once you'd used it.

Assignment, cleaned up

A couple of quiet fixes to who's-assigned-to-what: switching assignees now correctly hands the alert to the new person instead of leaving both names attached, and clicking Snooze or Bill Pulled no longer resets the assignment clock if you didn't actually change who's on it.
